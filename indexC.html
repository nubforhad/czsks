About Us

By the students, for the students of our district

Chuadanga Zila Student Welfare Association is a non-political, non-profit, volunteer-led organization. Students from Chuadanga Sadar, Alamdanga, Damurhuda and Jibannagar study across the country, and we bring them under one roof to support each other.

🎯
Our Mission

No talented student should stop studying because of financial hardship.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can become a member?＋

Any student from Chuadanga district studying at any institution.

Is there a membership fee?＋

No. Membership is free, and voluntary donations are welcome.

How do I apply for a scholarship?＋

Apply through the form once the notice is published on the board.

Can I volunteer without being a student?＋

Yes. Alumni and well-wishers are welcome as volunteers and mentors.
